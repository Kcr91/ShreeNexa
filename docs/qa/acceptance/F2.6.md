# F2.6 Acceptance Contract — Vectorized StrategyIR Compiler and Evaluator

## Feature

Vectorized compiler and batch evaluator for `StrategyIR` specifications, executing technical indicator dependency graphs, recursive signal grammar ASTs, time windows, and entry/exit signal generation over multi-bar historical datasets with strict G2 anti-lookahead guarantees.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Vector Strategy Compilation**:
  - `VectorStrategyCompiler`: Compiles a `StrategyIR` into an executable `CompiledStrategy`.
  - Automatically translates declared indicators into an `IndicatorDependencyGraph` and builds vectorized evaluation pipelines for all `entries` and `exits`.
- **Vectorized Signal Node Evaluation**:
  - `AndNode`, `OrNode`, `NotNode`: Vectorized element-wise boolean operations.
  - `IndicatorCompareNode`: Relational comparisons between price columns, indicator series, and constants.
  - `CrossOverNode` / `CrossUnderNode`: Strict bar-level crossings ($A_t > B_t \land A_{t-1} \le B_{t-1}$).
  - `PriceLevelBreakNode`: Price breakout above/below reference level, supporting `after` precondition gating.
  - `SequenceNode`: Multi-stage sequential condition fulfillment within a sliding window of $W$ bars.
  - `TimeWindowNode`: Time filtering supporting `clock` (e.g. `09:30-14:30`), `from_open`, `to_close`, and `dte`.
  - `PctChangeNode`: Rolling percentage change comparisons over $N$ bars.
  - `PersistNode`: Enforces that a child condition must evaluate to True for $K$ consecutive bars.
  - `RegimeNode` / `StrategySignalNode` / `CustomPythonNode`.
- **Evaluation Output Model**:
  - `StrategyEvaluationResult`:
    - `entry_signals: dict[str, list[bool]]` — Boolean entry trigger series per entry ID.
    - `exit_signals: dict[str, list[bool]]` — Boolean exit trigger series per exit ID.
    - `indicator_values: dict[str, list[Any]]` — Computed intermediate and indicator series.
    - `timestamps: list[datetime]` — Extracted or generated bar timestamps.
- **G2 Anti-Lookahead Audit**:
  - Evaluates strategy on a full $N$-bar dataset and truncated sub-datasets ($k < N$); proves that signals at timestamp $t$ are identical regardless of future bars.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/strategy/compiler.py` — `VectorStrategyCompiler`, `CompiledStrategy`, `StrategyEvaluationResult`.
2. `backend/app/strategy/__init__.py` — Package exports.
3. `docs/qa/acceptance/F2.6.md` — Acceptance contract.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Canonical ORB strategy evaluation
- Evaluates the worked example ORB strategy with RSI and EMA filters, producing correct entry signals.

### B2 — Signal AST operator correctness
- Validates `Sequence`, `Persist`, `CrossOver`, `CrossUnder`, `PriceLevelBreak` with `after`, `TimeWindow`, and `PctChange`.

### B3 — Determinism and repeatability (G3)
- Identical inputs and StrategyIR evaluate to identical boolean masks and signal indices.

### B4 — G2 truncated data anti-lookahead audit
- Proves signal values at bar $t$ are invariant under the addition of future bars $t+1 \dots t+k$.
