# F3.2 Acceptance Contract — SimBroker Fills and Slippage Models with Explicit Timing

## Feature

Simulated broker execution engine (`SimBroker`) supporting `next_bar_open` and `signal_bar_close` execution timings, market/limit/stop order matching against bar OHLC, pluggable slippage models (`NoSlippage`, `TickSlippage`, `PercentageSlippage`), and property-tested anti-lookahead and price containment invariants.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Fill Timings**:
  - `NEXT_BAR_OPEN` (Default): Signal generated at bar $T$ generates an order that executes on bar $T+1$ at Open price (with slippage). Strictly eliminates same-bar lookahead.
  - `SIGNAL_BAR_CLOSE`: Signal fills at bar $T$ Close price (with slippage, flagged in audit reports).
- **Slippage Models**:
  - `SlippageModel` abstract protocol.
  - `NoSlippageModel`: Zero slippage.
  - `TickSlippageModel`: Slippage by fixed tick multiples (e.g. 1 tick = ₹0.05).
  - `PercentageSlippageModel`: Slippage by fixed percentage (e.g. 0.05% for equities, 0.5% for options).
  - **Hard Invariant**: Fill price must strictly satisfy $bar.low \le P_{fill} \le bar.high$.
- **Order Matching Rules in `SimBroker`**:
  - `MARKET`: Fills at execution bar open (or close) ± slippage, clamped to $[bar.low, bar.high]$.
  - `LIMIT`:
    - BUY: Fills only if $bar.low \le limit\_price$.
    - SELL: Fills only if $bar.high \ge limit\_price$.
  - `SL_M` / `SL`:
    - BUY: Triggers if $bar.high \ge trigger\_price$.
    - SELL: Triggers if $bar.low \le trigger\_price$.
- **SimBroker Architecture**:
  - Implements `Broker` interface.
  - Placed in `backend/app/engine/sim_broker.py` (preserving live `broker.py` boundary).

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/engine/slippage.py` — Slippage models (`NoSlippageModel`, `TickSlippageModel`, `PercentageSlippageModel`).
2. `backend/app/engine/sim_broker.py` — `SimBroker`, `FillTiming`, and order matching engine.
3. `backend/app/engine/__init__.py` — Package exports.
4. `docs/qa/acceptance/F3.2.md` — Acceptance contract.
5. `backend/tests/unit/test_sim_broker.py` — Unit tests and property tests for fill timing, order types, slippage models, and $[low, high]$ containment.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Next-bar open fill timing and anti-lookahead
- Signals emitted at bar $T$ produce orders that do not execute on bar $T$; they execute on bar $T+1$ at Open price.

### B2 — Slippage models and price clamping invariant
- Applies tick-based and percentage-based slippage. Verifies with property/fuzz tests that no fill price ever falls below $bar.low$ or above $bar.high$.

### B3 — Limit and Stop order matching against bar OHLC
- Limit Buy at 105 fills when bar low is 104; does not fill when bar low is 106.
- Stop Loss Sell at 95 triggers and fills when bar low reaches 94.
