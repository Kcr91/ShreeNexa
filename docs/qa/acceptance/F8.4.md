# F8.4 Acceptance Contract — ATM IV, IV Rank/Percentile, OI/Volume PCR, Max Pain, Skew/Smile, and Term Structure

## Feature

Comprehensive options analytics suite including ATM IV derivation, historical IV rank and percentile with strict minimum-history rules, open interest and volume put-call ratios, max pain strike computation, IV skew/smile fitting with 25-delta risk reversal, term structure across expiries, and interactive UI visualization.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **ATM IV & Forward Moneyness (Spec §11.5)**:
  - Interpolates ATM IV at forward price $F$ using cubic or linear interpolation between adjacent strikes.
- **IV Rank & Percentile with Minimum-History Rules (Spec §11.5)**:
  - Explicit rule: Requires at least $N \ge 30$ historical trading days of IV observations.
  - If observations $< 30$, returns `is_valid: False` with `unreliable_reason = "Insufficient historical IV observations (requires >= 30 days)"` rather than fabricating percentiles.
  - Zero-spread division guard: When $\text{IV}_{\max} == \text{IV}_{\min}$, returns rank 50.0 without throwing divide-by-zero.
- **Max Pain Strike (Spec §11.5)**:
  - Identifies the strike $K^*$ where aggregate option buyers experience maximum cash loss at expiry.
  - Reconciled against independent mathematical hand fixtures.
- **PCR Ratios**:
  - Computes both $\text{PCR}_{\text{OI}} = \frac{\sum \text{Put OI}}{\sum \text{Call OI}}$ and $\text{PCR}_{\text{Volume}} = \frac{\sum \text{Put Vol}}{\sum \text{Call Vol}}$ with zero-denominator guards.
- **IV Skew and Smile Analysis**:
  - Extracts smile curve $\sigma(K)$ across strikes for front and monthly expiries.
  - Computes 25-delta Risk Reversal ($\sigma_{25\Delta\text{P}} - \sigma_{25\Delta\text{C}}$) and Butterfly curvature.
- **Term Structure**:
  - Maps ATM IV across sequential expiries $T_1, T_2, \dots, T_k$ to detect contango or backwardation regimes.
- **REST API & Frontend Visuals**:
  - `GET /api/v1/options/analytics/{underlying}` returns full analytics payload.
  - `OptionsAnalyticsPanel.tsx` renders Skew/Smile curve, Term Structure, Max Pain, and IV Rank gauges.
