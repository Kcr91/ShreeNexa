# F10.3 Acceptance Contract: Dividend Event Ledger, Matching, Withholding/Tax Metadata, and Income Views

## Feature Definition
- **Feature ID**: F10.3
- **Name**: Dividend event ledger, import, matching, withholding/tax metadata, and income views
- **Dependencies**: F10.1
- **Proof Requirement**: Recorded/hand fixtures; unmatched payment is reported rather than assigned to the wrong holding.

## Invariants & Acceptance Criteria

1. **Dividend Event Ledger**:
   - Maintains immutable records of declared, expected, and received dividends.
   - Fields: `dividend_id`, `account_id`, `security_id`, `isin`, `trading_symbol`, `record_date`, `ex_dividend_date`, `payment_date`, `rate_per_share`, `eligible_quantity`, `gross_amount`, `tds_rate_pct`, `tds_deducted`, `net_received`, `status`, and `notes`.
   - Thread-safe storage with support for filtering by account, security, date range, and match status.

2. **Eligible Quantity Calculation & Matching Engine**:
   - Calculates settled quantity held on `record_date` from active `TaxLot` tranches in `HoldingsLedger`.
   - Matches corporate declared dividend events or bank credit imports against holdings.
   - **Critical Invariant**: Unmatched dividend payments (e.g. payment received for an unheld security, zero shares on record date, or ISIN mismatch) must be explicitly flagged as `UNMATCHED` and reported in the unmatched report. They must **NEVER** be erroneously assigned to another security or holding.

3. **Tax & Withholding (TDS) Metadata**:
   - Supports Indian Income Tax Act Section 194 TDS tracking (standard 10% TDS above ₹5,000 threshold or user-specified exemption/higher rate).
   - Segregates gross income from net received cash, preserving Form 26AS reconciliation metadata.

4. **Income Views & Analytics**:
   - Monthly and annual income timeline aggregation.
   - Yield metrics:
     - **Yield on Cost (YoC)**: $\frac{\text{Annual Dividend}}{\text{Total Invested Capital}} \times 100\%$.
     - **Current Dividend Yield**: $\frac{\text{Annual Dividend}}{\text{Current Market Value}} \times 100\%$.
   - Upcoming/expected cashflow projections from declared future dividends.

5. **REST API Interface**:
   - `GET /api/v1/investing/dividends`: List recorded dividends with filters.
   - `POST /api/v1/investing/dividends/import`: Ingest dividend payment items.
   - `POST /api/v1/investing/dividends/match`: Execute matching engine against ledger holdings.
   - `GET /api/v1/investing/dividends/income-view`: Income calendar aggregation and yield metrics.
   - `GET /api/v1/investing/dividends/unmatched`: Audit view for unmatched payments.

6. **Quality & Boundary Gates**:
   - `mypy backend --strict`, `ruff check .`, `pytest`, frontend typecheck, test, build, and manifest validation.
   - Zero modifications to protected paths.
