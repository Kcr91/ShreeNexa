# F10.1 Acceptance Contract — Holdings Ledger, Lots, Average Cost, Corporate Actions, and Realised/Unrealised P&L Import/Reconciliation

## Feature

Long-term investment portfolio ledger tracking discrete tax lots, FIFO depletion, weighted-average acquisition costs, corporate actions (splits, bonuses, mergers), STCG/LTCG capital gains classification, and reconciliation against redacted Dhan holdings response fixtures.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Tax Lot Architecture and FIFO Depletion (§7.1, §7.2)**:
  - Each long-term holding consists of discrete purchase lots: `lot_id`, `security_id`, `isin`, `acquisition_date`, `acquisition_price`, `quantity`, `remaining_quantity`.
  - Disposals/sells deplete lots in strict First-In-First-Out (FIFO) order conforming to Section 45/48 of the Indian Income Tax Act.
  - Tax classification: Holdings held for $\ge 365$ calendar days (1 year) classify as Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG); holdings held for $< 365$ days classify as Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG).
- **Average Cost vs. FIFO**:
  - Both weighted-average cost (matching Dhan `/holdings` statements) and FIFO individual lot costs are maintained simultaneously.
  - Unrealised P&L is computed per holding using weighted average price vs. current market price:
    `unrealized_pnl = (cmp - avg_cost) * total_qty`.
  - Realised P&L is computed lot-by-lot against actual acquisition prices upon disposal.
- **Corporate Actions Without Return Distortion**:
  - **Invariant**: *Transfers and corporate actions do not masquerade as investment returns.*
  - **Stock Split (e.g. 1:2 split)**:
    - Multiplies each active lot's quantity by the split factor.
    - Divides each active lot's acquisition price by the split factor.
    - Total invested value remains unchanged; portfolio daily return is 0.0%.
  - **Bonus Issue (e.g. 1:1 bonus)**:
    - Issues new bonus shares with ₹0.00 acquisition price per share under Indian IT rules.
    - Blended average cost adjusts downward while total invested capital remains constant.
  - **Cash Dividend**:
    - Generates cash inflow without reducing holding shares or inflating price appreciation returns.
- **Broker Reconciliation Against Dhan Fixtures**:
  - Ingests Dhan-shaped `/holdings` payload containing `securityId`, `isin`, `tradingSymbol`, `totalQty`, `dpQty`, `avgCostPrice`.
  - Reconciles total quantity, demat delivery status, and weighted-average cost within ₹0.01 tolerance.
  - Flags discrepancies (unmatched quantities, price drift, external transfer-in).
- **REST API Endpoints**:
  - `GET /api/v1/investing/holdings`: List current holdings with weighted average cost, CMP, unrealised P&L, and lot breakdown.
  - `POST /api/v1/investing/lots`: Add manual/imported purchase lot.
  - `POST /api/v1/investing/disposals`: Record sale with FIFO lot depletion and STCG/LTCG breakdown.
  - `POST /api/v1/investing/corporate-actions`: Apply split, bonus, or consolidation.
  - `POST /api/v1/investing/reconcile-dhan`: Reconcile local holdings against Dhan `/holdings` JSON payload.
