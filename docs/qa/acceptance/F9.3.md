# F9.3 Acceptance Contract — Multiple Concurrent Paper Strategies with Isolated Capital and Shared Account Caps

## Feature

Multiple concurrent paper trading strategies running within an overarching paper account with strictly isolated capital and positions, governed by shared account-level risk caps and deterministic conflict resolution.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Isolated Strategy Books (Spec §9.3)**:
  - Each paper strategy is allocated a dedicated cash partition ($C_i$) from the parent account.
  - Strict cash isolation: A strategy's order submissions validate and draw only against its assigned cash balance. No order in Strategy A can draw from Strategy B's capital pool ($C_A \cap C_B = \emptyset$).
  - Strict position isolation: Open positions and inventory are strategy-scoped. If Strategy A is LONG 50 TCS and Strategy B is SHORT 20 TCS, both positions persist independently with dedicated average prices, MTM valuations, and realized P&L without cross-netting.
- **Shared Account Caps & Global Risk Layer**:
  - `max_single_stock_exposure_pct`: Shared constraint capping the combined gross exposure across all active strategies for any single security relative to total account equity.
  - `max_account_leverage`: Shared aggregate leverage cap (total gross positions / total equity).
  - `max_account_drawdown_pct`: Portfolio-level drawdown circuit breaker.
  - `kill_switch`: Global emergency halt stopping order placement across all strategies simultaneously.
- **Deterministic Conflict and Cap Behavior**:
  - Competing orders attempting to execute concurrently are evaluated deterministically in timestamp/sequence order.
  - Orders exceeding isolated capital are rejected with `Insufficient strategy capital`.
  - Orders breaching shared account caps are rejected with `Shared account cap breached: <detail>` without altering or corrupting the state of compliant strategies.
- **Tick and Bar Distribution**:
  - Market price ticks and bars fan out to all active strategy books, updating respective MTM valuations independently.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/paper/multi_strategy.py` — Multi-strategy coordinator, strategy books, shared cap models, and deterministic conflict handler.

3. `backend/tests/unit/test_multi_strategy_paper_isolation.py` — Comprehensive unit test proving zero cash/position leakage, deterministic conflict handling, and shared cap enforcement.
