# F6.2 Acceptance Contract — Combined Equity Curve, Drawdown, Aggregate Caps, and Marginal Contribution

## Feature

Portfolio-level combined equity curve generation, high water mark and drawdown time series, aggregate portfolio caps/risk guardrails, and marginal contribution to risk and return attribution.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Exact Reconciliation**:
  - Portfolio total cash and total equity at each time point $t$ must reconcile exactly to the sum of individual strategy-level fixtures:
    $E_{\text{port}}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^N E_i(t)$ and $\text{Cash}_{\text{port}}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^N \text{Cash}_i(t)$.
- **Drawdown and High Water Mark**:
  - $\text{HWM}(t) = \max_{s \le t} E_{\text{port}}(s)$.
  - Drawdown value $\text{DD}(t) = E_{\text{port}}(t) - \text{HWM}(t) \le 0$.
  - Drawdown percentage $\text{DD}_{\%}(t) = \frac{\text{DD}(t)}{\text{HWM}(t)}$.
  - Maximum Drawdown ($\text{MDD}$) and peak-to-trough duration calculation.
- **Aggregate Caps & Risk Guardrails**:
  - Portfolio Maximum Drawdown Cap: triggers a safety freeze or alerts when drawdown exceeds threshold.
  - Strategy Concentration Cap: enforces that no single strategy exceeds a maximum equity allocation percentage.
  - Leverage Cap: enforces Total Gross Notional / Total Equity $\le \text{MaxLeverage}$.
- **Marginal Contribution (Attribution)**:
  - Return contribution: $\sum_{i=1}^N \text{Contr}_{R,i} = R_{\text{port}}$.
  - Marginal Contribution to Risk (MCR):
    $\text{MCR}_i = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_i, R_{\text{port}})}{\sigma_{\text{port}}}$.
    $\text{Percentage Risk Contribution}_i = \frac{w_i \times \text{MCR}_i}{\sigma_{\text{port}}}$, strictly summing to 1.0 (100%).

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Combined equity and cash reconciliation
- Proves $E_{\text{port}}(t) = \sum E_i(t)$ and $\text{Cash}_{\text{port}}(t) = \sum \text{Cash}_i(t)$ across multi-strategy run.

### B2 — Portfolio drawdown and high water mark
- Computes drawdown curve, verifies $\text{DD}(t) \le 0$, validates max drawdown and peak/recovery dates against independent spreadsheet fixtures.

### B3 — Aggregate caps enforcement
- Verifies concentration cap detection and portfolio drawdown circuit breaker.

### B4 — Marginal contribution to risk and return
- Validates return contributions sum to portfolio total return.
- Validates percentage risk contributions $\sum \text{PCR}_i = 100\%$ within numerical tolerance.
