# F3.5 Acceptance Contract — Option Strategy Backtest Runner

## Feature

Multi-leg options strategy backtesting runner supporting dynamic Black-Scholes pricing, net Greeks aggregation ($\Delta, \Gamma, \Theta, \mathcal{V}, \rho$), SPAN/exposure margin requirements, effective-dated Indian option transaction costs (`F3.3`), and automated expiration exercise/assignment lifecycle.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Multi-Leg Structures**:
  - Supports Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread, Straddle, Strangle, Iron Condor, Iron Butterfly, and custom leg definitions.
  - Position sizing in contracts/lots.
- **Dynamic Pricing & Greeks**:
  - Prices options at every simulation step using `BlackScholesPricer` based on underlying spot, DTE ($T-t$), implied volatility $\sigma$, and risk-free rate $r$.
  - Aggregates net portfolio Greeks ($\Delta_{net}, \Gamma_{net}, \Theta_{net}, \mathcal{V}_{net}$).
- **Margin Model**:
  - Long options require premium outlay.
  - Short and multi-leg spread margin models calculating margin requirements and maximum risk.
- **Exercise & Expiration Lifecycle**:
  - Identifies ITM vs OTM status at contract expiration.
  - OTM options expire worthless (0.0 value).
  - ITM options are exercised/assigned at intrinsic value with accurate STT and settlement fees.
- **Manual Hand Calculation Reconciliation**:
  - Iron Condor and Bull Call Spread payoffs, net Greeks, margin, and expiration settlements reconcile to exact closed-form analytical formulas.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Bull Call Spread payoff and Greeks reconciliation
- Executes a 25000 / 25200 Bull Call Spread on NIFTY.
- Reconciles net $\Delta > 0$, net $\Theta < 0$, maximum upside payoff bounded by $(25200 - 25000 - \text{net\_debit}) \times \text{qty}$, and maximum loss equal to net debit paid.

### B2 — Iron Condor expiry and Greeks aggregation
- Executes 4-leg Iron Condor; validates net delta is near 0 (market neutral), net theta is positive (time decay harvest), and margin equals maximum wing width minus net credit.

### B3 — Expiration exercise and assignment handling
- Evaluates contracts at expiry date: ITM call settles at $\max(0, S - K)$, OTM call expires at 0.0 with position closed.
