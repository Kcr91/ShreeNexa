# F4.6 Acceptance Contract — Positions, Active Orders, and Trade Log Blotter Widget with Panic Button

## Feature

Integrated trading blotter widget managing real-time positions with live mark-to-market PnL calculations, active working orders management with individual and batch cancellation, historical execution trade log, and single-click "Cancel All Open Orders" panic action.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Three Dedicated Blotter Sub-Views**:
  - `Positions`: Displays open positions, instrument, side, net quantity, average buy price, live LTP, unrealized PnL, realized PnL, and square-off triggers.
  - `Open Orders`: Displays working orders, price, filled/total quantity, order status (OPEN, PENDING), and individual cancellation buttons.
  - `Trade Log`: Chronological audit log of completed fills and executions with timestamps, execution prices, and net traded values.
- **Mark-To-Market PnL Computation**:
  - Implements real-time unrealized PnL: `(ltp - buyAvgPrice) * quantity` for long positions, `(buyAvgPrice - ltp) * abs(quantity)` for short positions.
  - Computes aggregate portfolio summary metrics: Total Unrealized PnL, Total Realized PnL, Net PnL, and invested capital.
- **Panic Cancel-All Mechanism**:
  - "⚠️ Cancel All Orders" panic button immediately identifies all working/open orders, requests batch cancellation, and returns a verified cancellation receipt.
- **Widget Registry Integration**:
  - Registered in `widgetRegistry` under category `order`.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Real-time position mark-to-market PnL
- Updating the LTP of an open position dynamically recalculates both individual position unrealized PnL and total portfolio PnL.

### B2 — Working order management and individual cancel
- Active working orders display with status and allow canceling specific orders from the open orders list.

### B3 — Cancel-all panic button execution
- Triggering the panic button cancels all open and pending orders simultaneously in a single atomic action, transitioning them to CANCELLED and displaying a success receipt.
