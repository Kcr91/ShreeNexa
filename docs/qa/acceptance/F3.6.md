# F3.6 Acceptance Contract — Futures Strategy Backtest Runner

## Feature

Futures strategy backtesting runner supporting continuous contract stitching, automated calendar and volume-based roll execution, daily/continuous mark-to-market (MTM) accounting, SPAN/exposure initial margin validation, and effective-dated Indian regulatory futures taxation (`F3.3`).

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Futures Contract Lifecycle**:
  - Supports individual discrete monthly contracts and stitched synthetic continuous futures with configurable roll schedules (`days_before_expiry_roll`).
  - Executes roll transitions: closing near-month contract and opening next-month contract while accounting for roll spread, transaction fees, and STT.
- **Mark-to-Market & Margin**:
  - Continuous mark-to-market updating unrealized and realized PnL on every bar.
  - Computes initial and maintenance margin requirements (~10-15% of contract notional value).
- **Taxation & Fees**:
  - Applies Indian futures cost schedule (Brokerage min ₹20 / 0.03%, STT 0.0125% pre-Oct 2024 / 0.02% post-Oct 2024 on sell turnover, exchange charges, SEBI turnover fees, stamp duty on buy, 18% GST).
- **Analytical & Hand Reconciliation**:
  - Multi-month trend strategy rolling over consecutive contracts reconciles roll spread, basis, trade ledger, and final equity against hand calculations.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Futures contract rollover execution
- Long 1 lot NIFTY future in Month 1 rolled into Month 2 on roll date ($T_{exp} - 1$ day).
- Verifies closing fill on near contract (with sell STT), opening fill on next contract (with buy stamp duty), and accurate roll record with spread.

### B2 — Mark-to-market and margin requirement tracking
- Tracks intraday and multi-day price fluctuations; validates portfolio equity matches exact $(P_t - P_{entry}) \times \text{qty} - \text{costs}$.
- Verifies margin requirement is calculated as $\text{notional} \times \text{margin\_pct}$.

### B3 — Effective-dated futures taxation across 2024 tax boundary
- Evaluates futures sell trade pre-Oct 2024 (0.0125% STT) vs post-Oct 2024 (0.020% STT) during backtest run.
