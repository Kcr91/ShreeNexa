# F3.13 Acceptance Contract — P&L Calendar Widget and Trade Book Drill-Down

## Feature

Interactive P&L Calendar widget rendering monthly trading performance grids for Indian markets, color-coded daily profit/loss tiles, trading holiday and weekend indicators, monthly win-rate summaries, and comprehensive day-level trade book drill-down reconciliation.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Monthly Calendar Grid & Day Types**:
  - Full Indian market month grid with DayTypes: `TRADING_DAY`, `HOLIDAY` (NSE/BSE official holidays), and `WEEKEND`.
  - Color-coded daily tiles with profit/loss magnitude tinting.
- **Monthly Aggregate Summary**:
  - Total Gross PnL, Total Brokerage & Statutory Taxes (STT, Stamp Duty, GST, SEBI), Net PnL, Green/Red day counts, and Win Rate %.
- **Day Drill-Down Reconciliation**:
  - Clicking any active trading day tile opens the Day Trade Book inspector detailing individual executions, timestamps, traded instruments, and trade-level realized PnL.
- **Widget Registry Integration**:
  - Registered in `widgetRegistry` under category `analytics`.

## Required deliverables

1. `frontend/src/pnlcalendar/types.ts` — Type definitions for daily PnL records, holidays, and monthly summaries.
2. `frontend/src/pnlcalendar/calendar.ts` — Calendar grid computation, Indian holiday calendar mappings, and month aggregators.
3. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/PnlCalendarWidget.tsx` — Interactive PnL calendar grid with month navigation and trade drill-down pane.
4. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/index.ts` — Registration of `pnlCalendarDefinition`.
5. `frontend/src/pnlcalendar/calendar.test.ts` and `PnlCalendarWidget.test.tsx` — Unit tests.
6. `docs/qa/acceptance/F3.13.md` — Acceptance contract.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Monthly calendar grid rendering with holidays
- Renders full monthly calendar grid with trading days, weekends, and Indian exchange holidays.

### B2 — Month summary calculation
- Calculates total net PnL, green days, red days, and win rate % correctly from daily records.

### B3 — Day click trade book drill-down
- Clicking a day tile opens trade book drawer with individual executions matching the daily net PnL.
