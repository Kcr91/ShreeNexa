# F4.7 Acceptance Contract — Option Chain Widget with Greeks, IV, and Strike Selection

## Feature

Interactive Indian market option chain widget with dual-sided (Calls / Puts) strike ladder, real-time Black-Scholes Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega), Implied Volatility (IV), Open Interest (OI) metrics, Put-Call Ratio (PCR), Max Pain analysis, and one-click strike selection into order legs.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Symmetrical Option Ladder Structure**:
  - Center column displays Strike prices with ATM (At-The-Money) neon marker.
  - Left columns: Call options data (OI, OI change, IV, Delta, Theta, LTP, Bid, Ask).
  - Right columns: Put options data (LTP, Bid, Ask, Delta, Theta, IV, OI, OI change).
- **Greeks & Market Analytics**:
  - Closed-form Black-Scholes European pricing model for Indian Index options (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY).
  - Calculates Delta, Gamma, Theta (1-day decay in ₹), and Vega.
  - Computes aggregate Put-Call Ratio (PCR) and Max Pain strike.
- **Strike Selection & Order Ticket Integration**:
  - Clicking any call or put strike generates an `OptionLeg` and triggers selection receipt ready for multi-leg order execution.
- **Widget Registry Integration**:
  - Registered in `widgetRegistry` under category `analytics`.

## Required deliverables

1. `frontend/src/optionchain/types.ts` — Type definitions for option contracts, strike rows, chain data, and settings.
2. `frontend/src/optionchain/greeks.ts` — Black-Scholes Greeks computation and chain row generation.
3. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/OptionChainWidget.tsx` — Dual-sided option chain component with filters and leg selector.
4. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/index.ts` — Built-in registration of `optionChainDefinition`.
5. `frontend/src/optionchain/greeks.test.ts` and `OptionChainWidget.test.tsx` — Unit tests.
6. `docs/qa/acceptance/F4.7.md` — Acceptance contract.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Option chain strike ladder rendering
- Option chain renders symmetrical call and put columns around strike prices with clear ATM highlighting based on spot price.

### B2 — Greeks and IV calculation
- Greeks (Delta in [0, 1] for CE, [-1, 0] for PE, Theta negative, Gamma positive) and IV are computed accurately.

### B3 — Strike selection into order leg
- Clicking on a call or put row triggers leg selection and outputs formatted `OptionLeg` data.
