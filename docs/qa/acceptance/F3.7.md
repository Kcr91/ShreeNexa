# F3.7 Acceptance Contract — Portfolio Backtest Runner with Capital Allocation

## Feature

Portfolio-level backtesting coordinator enabling multi-strategy capital allocation, shared capital/margin pools, periodic and drift-based rebalancing, individual strategy contribution attribution, and aggregate portfolio equity curve and risk metrics generation.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Multi-Strategy Capital Allocation**:
  - Supports allocating a master capital pool across arbitrary sub-strategies (Equity/Stock, Options, and Futures) via explicit target weights ($\sum w_i = 1.0$).
  - Allocates pro-rata initial capital $C_i = C_{total} \times w_i$.
- **Aggregated Equity Curve & Shared Pool**:
  - Merges time series of daily equity curves into a single master portfolio equity curve $E_{total}(t) = \sum E_i(t)$.
  - Computes combined Sharpe ratio, Sortino, Calmar, Max Drawdown, CAGR, and total returns on the aggregate portfolio equity.
- **Strategy Contribution & Attribution**:
  - Decomposes total portfolio PnL into individual strategy contributions: absolute PnL, return %, initial weight vs final weight drift.
- **Rebalancing Engine**:
  - Evaluates periodic and tolerance-band (drift-based) rebalancing schedules, shifting capital to maintain target risk allocation.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Multi-strategy capital allocation and combined equity curve
- Combines Stock Strategy (60% weight) and Option Strategy (40% weight) with master capital ₹2,000,000.
- Verifies aggregate equity curve equals sum of component equities at every timestep.
- Verifies aggregate PnL and return match exact hand calculations.

### B2 — Multi-asset class portfolio (Stock + Option + Futures)
- Combines Stock Strategy (50%), Option Strategy (30%), and Futures Strategy (20%).
- Verifies strategy attribution table accurately calculates initial weight, final weight, individual PnL, and contribution.

### B3 — Drift-based rebalancing
- Simulates outperforming strategy causing allocation drift beyond 5% tolerance threshold.
- Triggers rebalance event and adjusts capital allocation back to target weights.
