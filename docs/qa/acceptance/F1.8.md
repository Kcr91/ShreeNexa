# F1.8 Acceptance Contract — Continuous Synthetic Futures Series Generator

## Feature

Continuous synthetic futures series generator for Indian index and stock futures (`NIFTY_F1`, `BANKNIFTY_F1`, etc.) supporting calendar, volume, and open-interest roll rules, with unadjusted, difference, and ratio roll-gap adjustment policies.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Contract Expiry & Roll Triggers**:
  - **Calendar Roll**: Triggers roll on or $N$ days prior to the active contract's expiry date (e.g. 0 days = on expiry day).
  - **Volume Roll**: Triggers roll on the first day where the next-month contract volume exceeds current contract volume ($\text{vol}_{\text{next}} > \text{vol}_{\text{curr}}$).
  - **Open Interest Roll**: Triggers roll on the first day where next-month OI exceeds current contract OI ($\text{oi}_{\text{next}} > \text{oi}_{\text{curr}}$).
- **Roll Adjustment Policies**:
  - `AdjustmentMethod.UNADJUSTED`: Pure nominal price series of the active contract stitched together with discrete price gaps at roll points.
  - `AdjustmentMethod.DIFFERENCE` (Panama back-adjustment): Additive backward shift by the roll spread $\Delta = P_{\text{new}} - P_{\text{old}}$ to eliminate price jumps while preserving dollar price movements.
  - `AdjustmentMethod.RATIO`: Multiplicative backward adjustment by $P_{\text{new}} / P_{\text{old}}$ preserving percentage returns.
- **Continuous Contract Identification**:
  - Generates standardized continuous symbols (e.g. `NIFTY_F1` for front-month, `NIFTY_F2` for near-month).
- **Roll Event Provenance**:
  - Every roll is logged with typed `RollEvent` metadata recording `roll_date`, `from_contract`, `to_contract`, `old_close`, `new_close`, `spread`, `ratio`, and `trigger_reason`.
- **Parity Proof**:
  - Verified on historical NIFTY / BANKNIFTY futures sample fixtures reconciling exact roll dates and adjusted price curves across all roll rules.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Calendar roll triggering
- Rolls from current to next contract exactly $N$ days prior to expiry.

### B2 — Volume-based roll triggering
- Detects the first date where next contract volume exceeds current contract volume and initiates the roll.

### B3 — Open interest roll triggering
- Detects the first date where next contract OI exceeds current contract OI and initiates the roll.

### B4 — Continuous price adjustment methods (Difference & Ratio)
- Verifies that Difference adjustment shifts prior bars by exact spread $\Delta$ removing price gaps.
- Verifies that Ratio adjustment scales prior bars by exact ratio $P_{\text{new}} / P_{\text{old}}$.

### B5 — Roll event audit recording
- Verifies all roll events are recorded with complete provenance metadata.
