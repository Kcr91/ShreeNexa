# F1.9 Acceptance Contract — Synthetic Continuous Option Surface Generator

## Feature

Synthetic continuous option surface generator (constant moneyness, constant maturity, BSM/Black-76 Implied Volatility solver, and analytical Greeks calculation engine).

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Black-Scholes-Merton & Black-76 Analytical Models**:
  - European pricing model supporting index options (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY) and equity options.
  - Formulas:
    $$d_1 = \frac{\ln(S/K) + (r - q + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}$$
    $$d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T}$$
- **Robust Implied Volatility (IV) Inversion**:
  - High-precision root solver using Newton-Raphson with bounded bisection fallback.
  - Accuracy: Inversion recovers option market price within $10^{-5}$ absolute error.
  - Boundary bounds: $\sigma \in [0.001, 5.0]$ (0.1% to 500% IV). Returns `None` or raises on non-arbitrageable/invalid market prices.
- **Analytical Option Greeks**:
  - **Delta ($\Delta$)**: Call $\Delta = e^{-qT}N(d_1)$, Put $\Delta = -e^{-qT}N(-d_1)$.
  - **Gamma ($\Gamma$)**: $\Gamma = \frac{e^{-qT} n(d_1)}{S \sigma \sqrt{T}}$ (identical for call and put).
  - **Vega ($\mathcal{V}$)**: $\mathcal{V} = \frac{S e^{-qT} n(d_1)\sqrt{T}}{100}$ (1% volatility change).
  - **Theta ($\Theta$)**: Daily calendar theta ($/ 365$).
  - **Rho ($\rho$)**: Sensitivity per 1% change in risk-free interest rate ($/ 100$).
- **Constant Moneyness & Constant Maturity Surfaces**:
  - Generates synthetic option surfaces at standardized moneyness points ($K/S \in [0.90, 0.95, 0.975, 1.0, 1.025, 1.05, 1.10]$).
  - Interpolates constant maturity slices (e.g. constant 7-day, 30-day, 60-day DTE) across discrete contract expiries.
- **Parity Proof**:
  - Independent reference fixture `backend/tests/fixtures/sample_option_surface_greeks.json` verified with zero discrepancy against analytical Black-Scholes ground truth.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Black-Scholes pricing and Put-Call parity
- Validates call and put pricing against analytical formulas and confirms exact put-call parity ($C - P = S e^{-qT} - K e^{-rT}$).

### B2 — High-precision IV inversion
- Recovers exact input volatility from option market price via Newton-Raphson / bisection solver.

### B3 — Analytical Greeks verification
- Validates Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, and Rho against the reference fixture values.

### B4 — Constant moneyness and maturity surface building
- Generates constant moneyness ATM and OTM slices across 30-day constant maturity DTE.
