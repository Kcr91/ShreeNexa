# F3.8 Acceptance Contract — Monte Carlo and Walk-Forward Analysis

## Feature

Robustness and validation framework providing deterministic Monte Carlo trade/bar resampling, confidence intervals, risk of ruin analytics, rolling/anchored walk-forward optimization splits, and out-of-sample Walk-Forward Efficiency (WFE) evaluation.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Monte Carlo Resampling**:
  - Resampling modes: Trade Shuffle (`TRADE_SHUFFLE`), Bootstrap Resampling (`BOOTSTRAP`), and Block Bootstrap (`BLOCK_BOOTSTRAP`).
  - Computes distribution percentiles ($P_5, P_{25}, P_{50}, P_{75}, P_{95}, P_{99}$) for terminal equity, maximum drawdown %, and Sharpe ratio.
  - Calculates empirical risk of ruin ($\Pr(\text{Equity}_t \le \text{ruin\_threshold})$).
  - Deterministic PRNG seeding for reproducible audit runs.
- **Walk-Forward Analysis (WFA)**:
  - Generates anchored (expanding) or rolling In-Sample (IS) training and Out-of-Sample (OOS) validation windows.
  - Stitches contiguous OOS performance equity curves across all validation windows.
  - Calculates Walk-Forward Efficiency ($\text{WFE} = \frac{\text{CAGR}_{OOS}}{\text{CAGR}_{IS}}$) per window and mean overall WFE.
  - Computes robustness score (% of OOS validation windows profitable).
- **Verification & Exactness**:
  - Reconciles percentile ranks, mean WFE, and window date splits against independent hand calculations.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Deterministic Monte Carlo simulation and percentile reconciliation
- Runs 1,000 Monte Carlo trade reshuffles with known deterministic seed.
- Reconciles $P_5, P_{50}, P_{95}$ terminal equity and maximum drawdown % against hand-calculated percentile formulas.
- Validates Risk of Ruin calculation.

### B2 — Walk-Forward split generation (Rolling & Anchored)
- Evaluates rolling vs anchored window partition over a multi-month period.
- Verifies exact datetime boundaries for training and validation splits with zero lookahead overlap.

### B3 — Walk-Forward Analysis and stitched Out-of-Sample equity
- Runs 3-window Walk-Forward Analysis on stock strategy.
- Verifies individual window WFE ratios ($\text{CAGR}_{OOS} / \text{CAGR}_{IS}$) and stitched contiguous OOS equity curve.
