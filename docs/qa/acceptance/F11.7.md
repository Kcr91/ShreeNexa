# F11.7 Acceptance Contract: Approval-Gated Blue/Green Promotion, Health Check, Drain, Rollback, and History

## Feature Definition
- **Feature ID**: F11.7
- **Name**: Approval-gated blue/green promotion, health check, drain, rollback, and history.
- **Dependencies**: F11.6, F13.1, F13.2, F13.3
- **Proof Requirement**: Promote/rollback during an active paper strategy; engine is not restarted; audit is complete.

## Invariants & Acceptance Criteria

1. **Explicit Operator Approval Gate**:
   - Automated runs and sandbox validations cannot unilaterally promote to production.
   - Promotion workflow requires explicit operator approval (`POST /api/v1/feature-builder/promotion/approve`).
   - Unapproved candidates cannot trigger proxy flipping or traffic redirection.

2. **Pre-Traffic Health Check & Gating**:
   - Before traffic is switched, candidate port (8000 or 8001) is health checked via `GET /healthz`.
   - If candidate fails health check, promotion is immediately aborted, Caddy upstream remains untouched on active instance, and a failure alert is recorded.

3. **Caddy Proxy Flip**:
   - Upstream in Caddyfile is updated atomically to point to candidate port (Blue 8000 <-> Green 8001).
   - Zero dropped packets or downtime during the switch.

4. **Graceful Connection Drain**:
   - The previously active API process enters a graceful drain window, allowing in-flight requests and connections to complete without dropping.

5. **Engine Continuity & Process Independence Invariant**:
   - **Crucial Invariant**: Throughout candidate start, health check, proxy flip, drain, and any rollback, the trading `engine` process (`shreenexa-engine`), paper deployments, and background workers remain completely undisturbed and continuously running.
   - Running paper strategies, order books, and positions in `engine` suffer zero disruption or restart.
   - PID and heartbeat of `engine` are verified before and after promotion/rollback.

6. **Instant Rollback Capability**:
   - `POST /api/v1/feature-builder/promotion/rollback`:
     - Flips Caddy reverse proxy upstream back to the previous color.
     - Candidate is safely stopped/demoted.
     - `engine` remains running without interruption.
     - Full rollback record is written to deployment history.

7. **Deployment History & Audit Trail**:
   - Complete, immutable audit log of all promotion requests, operator approvals, health check outputs, proxy switches, drain states, and rollbacks (`GET /api/v1/feature-builder/promotion/history`).

8. **Proof Suite (`backend/tests/unit/test_feature_builder_promotion.py`)**:
   - Proves promotion execution during an active paper strategy:
     - Confirms paper strategy running on engine.
     - Captures engine PID and uptime.
     - Promotes candidate (Blue -> Green) with approval and passing health probe.
     - Confirms engine PID and running strategy are 100% unchanged.
   - Proves rollback execution:
     - Rolls back Green -> Blue.
     - Confirms engine PID and paper strategy remain 100% unchanged.
   - Proves unapproved or unhealthy promotion is blocked.
   - Proves complete audit history logging.
