# F1.6 Acceptance Contract — Session-Aware Bar Resampling

## Feature

Session-aware bar resampling from 1-minute historical data into higher timeframes (`3m`, `5m`, `15m`, `25m`, `30m`, `60m`, `1d`, `1w`), strict session-aligned bucket boundaries (09:15 IST), partial-bar policies, and mathematical OHLCV volume/OI invariant preservation.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Session-Aligned Intraday Bucketing**:
  - Intraday buckets align to the segment's session start (e.g. 09:15 IST for `NSE_EQ` / `NSE_FNO`) rather than wall-clock midnight or top-of-hour.
  - 5m buckets: `09:15-09:20`, `09:20-09:25`, ..., `15:25-15:30`.
  - 15m buckets: `09:15-09:30`, `09:30-09:45`, ..., `15:15-15:30`.
  - 60m buckets: `09:15-10:15`, `10:15-11:15`, ..., `14:15-15:15`, `15:15-15:30` (partial bar).
- **Mathematical OHLCV Aggregation Invariants**:
  - `open`: first bar's open in the bucket.
  - `high`: maximum high across all bars in the bucket (`max(high)`).
  - `low`: minimum low across all bars in the bucket (`min(low)`).
  - `close`: last bar's close in the bucket.
  - `volume`: exact sum of volume across all bars (`sum(volume)`).
  - `open_interest`: last bar's open interest (`last(oi)`).
  - Preserves volume conservation: $\sum \text{volume}_{1m} = \sum \text{volume}_{\text{resampled}}$.
  - Preserves high/low bounds: $\text{high} \ge \max(\text{open}, \text{close})$ and $\text{low} \le \min(\text{open}, \text{close})$.
- **Partial Bar Policy**:
  - `PartialBarPolicy.EMIT_PARTIAL` (default): Emits partial final session bars (e.g., the 15-minute 15:15–15:30 bar on a 60m aggregation).
  - `PartialBarPolicy.DROP_INCOMPLETE`: Discards incomplete buckets.
- **Multi-Day & Weekly Bucketing**:
  - Daily (`1d`): Single bar per active trading day covering full session bounds.
  - Weekly (`1w`): Aggregates all session bars for a calendar week (Monday through Friday/Sunday).

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Timeframe parsing and validation
- Parses standard timeframe strings (`3m`, `5m`, `15m`, `25m`, `30m`, `60m`, `1d`, `1w`).
- Rejects invalid timeframe specifiers.

### B2 — 5m and 15m session alignment
- Resamples a full day of 1m bars into 5m bars (75 bars total: 09:15 to 15:30 IST) and 15m bars (25 bars total).
- Verifies exact session alignment starting at 09:15 IST.

### B3 — 60m resampling and partial closing bar
- Resamples a full day into 60m bars; produces 6 full 60m bars + 1 15m closing bar (`15:15-15:30`).
- Verifies `EMIT_PARTIAL` emits 7 bars and `DROP_INCOMPLETE` emits 6 bars.

### B4 — Volume conservation and OHLC bounds invariants
- Validates that total volume across all resampled bars equals the sum of 1m bar volumes with zero loss or inflation.
- Validates that daily high/low matches the maximum high and minimum low across all underlying 1m bars.

### B5 — Daily and Weekly resampling
- Resamples multiple days into daily `1d` bars and weekly `1w` bars with proper date timestamps.
