# F7.7 Acceptance Contract — Session-Aware Live One-Minute Bar Builder

## Feature

Session-aware live one-minute bar builder merged onto warehouse history, handling late, duplicate, and out-of-order ticks, and reconciling with Dhan minute bars.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Session Alignment (ADR-0003, F1.1, F1.6)**:
  - 1-minute buckets align to canonical session bounds: e.g. for `NSE_EQ` and `NSE_FNO`, trading starts at 09:15:00 IST (03:45:00 UTC).
  - Bucket start timestamps are exact floor minute boundaries: `[09:15:00, 09:16:00)`, `[09:16:00, 09:17:00)`, ..., `[15:29:00, 15:30:00)`.
  - Ticks occurring outside session hours are rejected or partitioned if `enforce_session=True`.
- **Tick Invariants & Robustness (L2 / G2)**:
  - **Duplicate ticks**: Identical tick signatures `(timestamp, price, volume, sequence)` are deduplicated. Double-counting of volume is strictly prevented.
  - **Out-of-order ticks**: If a tick arrives whose timestamp is earlier than the latest processed tick within the bucket:
    - High/low bounds are updated: $\text{high} = \max(\text{high}, \text{price})$, $\text{low} = \min(\text{low}, \text{price})$.
    - Open price reflects the earliest tick by timestamp; close price reflects the latest tick by timestamp.
    - Volume is accumulated properly.
  - **Late ticks & Grace Window**:
    - Finalized bars maintain a configurable grace window (default 15s).
    - Ticks arriving within the grace window for the immediately preceding bar retroactively update the finalized bar.
    - Ticks arriving beyond the grace window are dropped and tracked in telemetry.
- **Merging with Warehouse History**:
  - `merge_history_and_live(warehouse_bars, live_bars)` creates a unified continuous sequence.
  - Dedup: Live bars supersede historical bars for any overlapping timestamps.
  - Invariant: Resulting bar sequence is strictly monotonically increasing by timestamp without duplicate or missing bars.
- **Dhan Minute Bar Reconciliation (L2)**:
  - Feeding a sequence of simulated or Dhan recorded ticks into `LiveBarBuilder` produces 1-minute bars matching Dhan's canonical minute bars with 100% exact numerical match.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/feedd/bar_builder.py` — `LiveTick`, `LiveMinuteBar`, `LiveBarBuilder`, session calendar integration, deduplication, out-of-order/late tick handling, and `merge_history_and_live`.
2. `backend/app/feedd/__init__.py` — Exports for `LiveBarBuilder`, `LiveTick`, `LiveMinuteBar`, `merge_history_and_live`.
3. `docs/qa/acceptance/F7.7.md` — Acceptance contract.
4. `backend/tests/unit/test_live_bar_builder.py` — Comprehensive unit tests:
   - Dhan 1-minute bar exact reconciliation.
   - Duplicate tick rejection without volume inflation.
   - Out-of-order tick handling (correct open, high, low, close).
   - Late tick grace window updates and dropped tick telemetry.
   - Session boundary alignment (09:15 to 15:30 IST).
   - Seamless merging with warehouse historical bars.
