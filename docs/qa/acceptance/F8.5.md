# F8.5 Acceptance Contract — Multi-Leg Option Strategy Builder with Expiry/T+n Payoff, Breakevens, Extrema, and Position Greeks

## Feature

Interactive and analytical multi-leg option strategy builder supporting arbitrary leg combinations, standard structural templates (Iron Condor, Straddle, Strangle, Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread, Iron Butterfly), expiration and T+n target date payoff curves, exact analytical breakeven computation, maximum profit / maximum loss extrema detection, and net aggregated position Greeks scaled by Indian market lot sizes.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Payoff & Extrema Aggregation Properties (Spec §11.6)**:
  - Expiry PnL for leg $i$:
    - Long Call: $Q_i \cdot \max(0, S_T - K_i) - Q_i \cdot P_{0, i}$
    - Short Call: $Q_i \cdot P_{0, i} - Q_i \cdot \max(0, S_T - K_i)$
    - Long Put: $Q_i \cdot \max(0, K_i - S_T) - Q_i \cdot P_{0, i}$
    - Short Put: $Q_i \cdot P_{0, i} - Q_i \cdot \max(0, K_i - S_T)$
    where $Q_i = \text{lots}_i \times \text{lot\_size}_i$.
  - Total Expiry PnL is exact linear summation across all active/enabled legs.
- **T+n Target Date Payoff Curve**:
  - Re-evaluates each option leg at future time $t_{\text{target}}$ using Black-76 pricer with remaining time to expiry $T - t_{\text{target}}$.
  - Accounts for spot drift and theta/time decay before expiration.
- **Breakeven Determination**:
  - Solves for all spot prices $S^*$ where aggregate Expiry PnL equals zero.
- **Extrema (Max Profit, Max Loss, Net Debit/Credit)**:
  - Detects whether upside/downside is bounded or unlimited.
  - Computes exact finite Max Profit, Max Loss, and Risk:Reward ratio.
- **Net Position Greeks**:
  - Net Delta $\Delta_{\text{net}} = \sum \text{sign}_i \cdot Q_i \cdot \Delta_i$
  - Net Gamma $\Gamma_{\text{net}} = \sum \text{sign}_i \cdot Q_i \cdot \Gamma_i$
  - Net Theta $\Theta_{\text{net}} = \sum \text{sign}_i \cdot Q_i \cdot \Theta_i$ (in ₹/day)
  - Net Vega $\mathcal{V}_{\text{net}} = \sum \text{sign}_i \cdot Q_i \cdot \mathcal{V}_i$ (in ₹/1% vol)
- **Interactive UI**:
  - Live table allowing adding, removing, disabling/enabling legs, changing strikes, quantities, actions, and premiums.
  - Instant template selection.
  - Visual interactive SVG Payoff Chart showing Expiry vs T+n lines, Breakevens, Max Profit/Loss, and Greeks summary.
