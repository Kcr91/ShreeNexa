# F3.10 Acceptance Contract — Metric Grading, Horizon Profiles, and Deployment Gates

## Feature

Automated strategy grading and scorecard engine supporting four horizon profiles (`INTRADAY`, `SWING`, `POSITIONAL`, `INVESTMENT`), multi-metric scoring bands (`EXCELLENT`, `GOOD`, `ACCEPTABLE`, `POOR`, `REJECTED`), style-specific win rate evaluation, automated risk flags, deployment gate validation, config versioning, and strategy verdict generation (`DEPLOYABLE`, `INVESTIGATE`, `REJECT`).

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Four Horizon Profiles**:
  - `INTRADAY`: Tight drawdown constraints (Max DD $\le 5\%$), high Sharpe requirement ($\ge 1.5$), high trade count.
  - `SWING`: Moderate drawdown ($\le 15\%$), Sharpe ($\ge 1.2$), multi-day holding.
  - `POSITIONAL`: Drawdown tolerance ($\le 25\%$), Sharpe ($\ge 1.0$).
  - `INVESTMENT`: Long-term compounding focus (CAGR, Calmar, Max DD $\le 30\%$).
- **Multi-Metric Scoring**:
  - Evaluates Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Calmar Ratio, Max Drawdown %, Win Rate %, Profit Factor, and CAGR %.
  - Assigns categorical grades and normalized 0–100 composite score based on versioned configuration thresholds.
- **Automated Risk Flags**:
  - `OVERFITTING_DETECTED` (from DSR / PBO audit).
  - `LOW_TRADE_COUNT` ($< 30$ trades).
  - `EXCESSIVE_DRAWDOWN` (breaching horizon limit).
  - `POOR_RISK_REWARD` (Profit Factor $< 1.1$).
- **Deployment Gates & Verdicts**:
  - `DEPLOYABLE`: All deployment gates passed and no critical flags.
  - `INVESTIGATE`: Passed basic performance but flagged for potential overfitting, borderline trade count, or high drawdown.
  - `REJECT`: Failed mandatory risk gates (negative return, extreme drawdown, unacceptable profit factor).

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Profile-specific metric grading and contiguous boundary properties
- Evaluates identical performance metrics across `INTRADAY` vs `POSITIONAL` profiles; verifies appropriate horizon-dependent grading adjustments.
- Verifies contiguous score boundary properties (no gaps or overlaps between EXCELLENT, GOOD, ACCEPTABLE, POOR, REJECTED).

### B2 — Overfitting result transition to INVESTIGATE
- Evaluates high-performing strategy that has an overfitting warning from DSR/PBO; verifies overall verdict automatically transitions from `DEPLOYABLE` to `INVESTIGATE`.

### B3 — Mandatory deployment gate rejection
- Evaluates strategy failing maximum drawdown or profit factor gate; verifies verdict is `REJECT` and failing gate reasons are reported explicitly.
