# F9.5 Acceptance Contract — Same-Session Paper-vs-Backtest Divergence Report

## Feature

Generate automated, multi-dimensional divergence reports comparing same-session live paper trading execution against replay backtests across signals, timestamps, execution prices, fills, costs, and P&L.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Six Comparative Dimensions (Spec §10.8, §14)**:
  1. **Signals**: Comparison of signal triggers (timestamps, symbols, directions, indicator values). Detects missed or unexpected signals.
  2. **Timestamps & Latency**: Measures execution timing delta ($\Delta t = t_{\text{paper}} - t_{\text{backtest}}$) between signal generation and fill confirmation.
  3. **Execution Prices**: Evaluates price variation and slippage drift ($P_{\text{paper}}$ vs $P_{\text{backtest}}$).
  4. **Fills & Quantities**: Detects partial fills, dropped orders, or quantity mismatches.
  5. **Transaction Costs**: Reconciles modeled vs realized statutory taxes, brokerage, and slippage.
  6. **P&L & Returns**: Reconciles gross P&L, net P&L, final equity, and max drawdown.
- **Divergence Classification & Severity**:
  - `PERFECT_MATCH`: Identical signals, fills, prices, and costs within narrow zero-drift tolerances.
  - `ACCEPTABLE_DRIFT`: Executions match within declared operational tolerances (e.g. realistic slippage $\le 0.1\%$, latency $\le 2$ seconds).
  - `DIVERGENCE_DETECTED`: Actionable execution discrepancies (e.g. slippage breach, dropped fill, partial execution, timing drift).
  - `CRITICAL_MISMATCH`: Fundamental execution failure (e.g. opposite side trades, unhedged risk, severe P&L discrepancy $> 2\%$).
- **Root-Cause Attribution**:
  - Every identified discrepancy item is annotated with a structured category (`SLIPPAGE_DISCREPANCY`, `LATENCY_DELAY`, `MISSED_SIGNAL`, `UNEXPECTED_SIGNAL`, `PARTIAL_FILL`, `COST_MODEL_DRIFT`, `REJECTED_ORDER`) and human-readable explanation.
- **Proof Invariant**:
  - Known injected divergences (price slippage, delay, missed signal, dropped fill) must be accurately detected, localized to the specific order/trade, and attributed with the correct discrepancy category.
  - Identical inputs must reconcile cleanly within declared tolerances with verdict `PERFECT_MATCH` or `ACCEPTABLE_DRIFT`.
- **API & Serialization**:
  - `POST /api/v1/paper/divergence-report`: Generates and returns a comprehensive `SessionDivergenceReport`.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/paper/divergence.py` — Engine for computing same-session divergence reports, tolerances, discrepancy attribution, and summary verdicts.
2. `backend/app/api/paper.py` — REST endpoint exposing `/api/v1/paper/divergence-report`.
3. `backend/app/paper/__init__.py` — Package exports for divergence models and functions.
4. `backend/tests/unit/test_paper_backtest_divergence.py` — Unit tests verifying known injected divergence detection, exact localization, clean identity reconciliation, and API integration.
5. `docs/qa/acceptance/F9.5.md` — Acceptance contract.
