# F3.3 Acceptance Contract — Effective-Dated Indian Cost Model

## Feature

Comprehensive Indian market transaction cost model with effective-date range support (`config/costs.yaml`), accurately calculating Brokerage, STT/CTT, Exchange Transaction Charges, SEBI Turnover Fees, Stamp Duty, and 18% GST across Equity Delivery, Intraday, Futures, and Options for historical and present tax regimes (including the October 1, 2024 STT & true-to-label fee updates).

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Tax & Regulatory Components**:
  - `Brokerage`: Standard Dhan flat ₹20/order or 0.03% (zero for equity delivery).
  - `STT / CTT`:
    - Delivery: 0.1% on both Buy and Sell turnover.
    - Intraday: 0.025% on Sell turnover.
    - Futures: 0.0125% on Sell turnover prior to 2024-10-01; 0.02% from 2024-10-01.
    - Options: 0.0625% on Sell premium prior to 2024-10-01; 0.1% on Sell premium from 2024-10-01.
  - `Exchange Transaction Charges`: Per-segment rate on turnover / premium.
  - `SEBI Turnover Fee`: ₹10 per crore (0.0001%).
  - `Stamp Duty`: Buy-side only (Delivery: 0.015%, Intraday: 0.003%, Futures: 0.002%, Options: 0.003%).
  - `GST`: 18% on `(Brokerage + Exchange Txn Charges + SEBI Fee)`.
- **Effective-Dated Schedule**:
  - `config/costs.yaml` defines schedules with `effective_from` and optional `effective_to` dates.
  - Backtests evaluate the legal rate schedule in effect on the exact historical trade date.
- **Contract Note Reconciliation**:
  - Reconciles against a sanitized fixture representing a real Dhan contract note with exact line-item breakdowns.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Effective-date schedule resolution
- A futures sell trade in September 2024 incurs 0.0125% STT; an identical trade in October 2024 incurs 0.02% STT.
- An options sell trade in September 2024 incurs 0.0625% STT; in October 2024 incurs 0.1% STT.

### B2 — Segment and side specific cost calculation
- Equity Delivery: Stamp duty (0.015%) charged on Buy only; STT (0.1%) charged on Buy and Sell; Brokerage is ₹0.
- Equity Intraday: Stamp duty (0.003%) on Buy only; STT (0.025%) on Sell only; Brokerage is min(₹20, 0.03% turnover).
- GST is strictly 18% of `(brokerage + exchange_txn + sebi_fee)`.

### B3 — Redacted contract note reconciliation
- Reconciles line-by-line against `sample_contract_note.json` with rounding parity $\le ₹0.02$.
