# F13.5 Acceptance Contract: Uptime, Feed Freshness, Disk, Token Expiry, Queue, Engine, Backup, and Data-Gap Monitoring with Actionable Alerts

## Feature Definition
- **Feature ID**: F13.5
- **Name**: Uptime, feed freshness, disk, token expiry, queue, engine, backup, and data-gap monitoring with actionable alerts.
- **Dependencies**: F13.1, F13.2, F13.3, F13.4
- **Proof Requirement**: Injected failures trigger one clear alert and recovery notice; stale feed cannot look healthy.

## Invariants & Acceptance Criteria

1. **Subsystem Coverage (`backend/app/monitoring/`)**:
   - **Uptime & Service Health**: Supervised health tracking across independent processes (`api`, `engine`, `feedd`, `worker`, `redis`, `postgres`, `caddy`).
   - **Feed Freshness**: Continuous latency and packet interval tracking. A feed with no packets exceeding the threshold (> 2.0s warning, > 5.0s critical during market hours) transitions to `STALE` or `CRITICAL`.
   - **Disk Space**: Filesystem space monitoring with configurable warning (< 15%) and critical (< 5%) thresholds.
   - **Token Expiry**: Dhan auth token lifespan check warning at < 24 hours and critical at < 2 hours.
   - **Queue Backpressure**: Ingestion and fanout buffer monitoring detecting queue backpressure and memory bloat.
   - **Engine Trading Health**: Engine process heartbeat monitoring detecting execution stall or unhandled faults.
   - **Backup Freshness**: Validates that a successful backup snapshot exists within the last 26 hours.
   - **Data-Gap Detection**: Detects missing minute bars during active Indian market trading hours (09:15–15:30 IST).

2. **Actionable Alerting & De-duplication**:
   - **Stateful Alert Lifecycle**: Alerts transition through `PENDING` -> `FIRING` -> `RESOLVED`.
   - **De-duplication**: Repeated failures in a flapping check do not spam notifications; exactly **one** alert is dispatched until state changes.
   - **Recovery Notices**: When a failing subsystem recovers and satisfies healthy thresholds, an explicit `RESOLVED` recovery notification is dispatched with diagnostic metrics.
   - **Actionable Remediation**: Every alert payload provides clear remediation steps (e.g., "Run token renewal CLI", "Prune logs or extend disk", "Trigger backfill worker").

3. **Stale Feed Invariant**:
   - *A stale feed cannot look healthy.* Any synthetic or injected delay in packet timestamps immediately degrades the feed status and triggers an actionable alert.

4. **REST API Interface**:
   - `GET /api/v1/monitoring/health` -> System-wide aggregate status and itemized subsystem reports.
   - `GET /api/v1/monitoring/alerts` -> Active firing alerts and historical audit log.
   - `POST /api/v1/monitoring/alerts/acknowledge` -> Operator acknowledgment.

5. **Verification & Proof Suite (`backend/tests/unit/test_monitoring_system.py`)**:
   - Injected failure triggers exactly one clear alert.
   - Restoring subsystem health triggers a corresponding recovery notice (`RESOLVED`).
   - Verifies that a feed exceeding freshness threshold is marked `STALE`/`CRITICAL` and never reports as healthy.
   - Covers all 8 monitored domains: uptime, feed freshness, disk, token expiry, queue, engine, backup, and data gaps.
