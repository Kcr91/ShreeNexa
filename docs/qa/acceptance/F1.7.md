# F1.7 Acceptance Contract — Corporate Action Adjustment Pipeline

## Feature

Corporate action adjustment pipeline (stock splits, bonus issues, cash dividends, rights issues) with point-in-time preservation, cumulative factor computation, and unadjusted data immutability.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Immutability Invariant**:
  - Source raw ingests (`data/raw/`) and published unadjusted warehouse partitions (`data/warehouse/`) remain completely unadjusted and immutable.
  - Corporate action adjustments are computed as an overlay/transformer on demand or published as distinct adjusted views.
- **Supported Corporate Actions & Factor Formulations**:
  - **Stock Split**: Ratio $A:B$ (e.g., 1 share of FV 10 splits into 5 shares of FV 2 $\implies$ ratio 1:5).
    - Adjustment factor $F = A / B = 1 / 5 = 0.2$.
    - Price $\text{adj} = \text{price} \times F$; Volume $\text{adj} = \text{round}(\text{volume} / F)$.
  - **Bonus Issue**: Ratio $A:B$ ($A$ bonus shares for every $B$ held $\implies$ e.g. 1:1 bonus).
    - Adjustment factor $F = B / (A + B) = 1 / 2 = 0.5$.
    - Price $\text{adj} = \text{price} \times F$; Volume $\text{adj} = \text{round}(\text{volume} / F)$.
  - **Cash Dividend**: Dividend per share $D$.
    - Dividend adjustment factor $F = (\text{close}_{\text{pre}} - D) / \text{close}_{\text{pre}}$.
  - **Rights Issue**: Issue price $P_{\text{issue}}$, ratio $A:B$.
- **Multi-Event Cumulative Adjustments**:
  - Multiple historical corporate actions for a single security compound cumulatively backwards in time.
  - Bars on or after the ex-date are not adjusted ($F = 1.0$).
  - Bars prior to the ex-date are scaled by the cumulative product of subsequent corporate action factors.
- **Preservation of Point-in-Time History**:
  - Backtests and analytical readers can query both nominal unadjusted prices (for live trade settlement and cash-flow accounting) and adjusted prices (for continuous technical analysis and indicator calculations).

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Stock split factor math and volume scaling
- 1:5 split on ex-date adjusts prior prices by 0.2 and scales volume by 5x; post ex-date prices are untouched.

### B2 — Bonus issue factor math
- 1:1 bonus issue on ex-date adjusts prior prices by 0.5 and doubles prior volume; post ex-date prices are untouched.

### B3 — Chained multi-event cumulative adjustment
- Multiple events (e.g. 1:1 bonus in 2024 followed by 1:5 split in 2026) combine cumulatively ($0.5 \times 0.2 = 0.1$) for data prior to the first event.

### B4 — Dividend adjustment
- Computes proportional dividend factor and adjusts historical OHLC prices while preserving nominal close for cash accounting.

### B5 — Unadjusted data immutability
- Verifies that applying adjustments produces a new transformed dataset and leaves the original BarRecord list and source Parquet partitions 100% unaltered.
