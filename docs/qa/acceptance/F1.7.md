# F1.7 Acceptance Contract — Data-Quality Reporting

## Feature

Comprehensive data-quality reporting engine across the historical warehouse for gaps, duplicates, price outliers, zero volume, unexpected dates/holidays, stale partitions, and universe/date coverage metrics, with distinct classification of upstream source gaps versus warehouse storage errors.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Defect Categories & Classifications**:
  - `TIMESTAMP_GAP`: Missing trading bars during active market hours according to `TradingCalendar`.
  - `DUPLICATE_TIMESTAMP`: Multiple bars with identical timestamp for the same security.
  - `PRICE_OUTLIER`: Price spikes/flash anomalies exceeding standard statistical volatility envelope.
  - `ZERO_VOLUME_ACTIVE`: Zero volume during active regular trading sessions.
  - `UNEXPECTED_SESSION_DATE`: Bars logged on official holidays, weekends, or outside segment session bounds.
  - `STALE_PARTITION`: Partitions missing recent market dates or exhibiting incomplete symbol coverage against universe.
- **Classification Separation**:
  - `OriginCategory.UPSTREAM_SOURCE`: Gaps/omissions present in raw broker payloads.
  - `OriginCategory.WAREHOUSE_INTEGRITY`: Corrupt Parquet records, duplicate ingestion, or manifest mismatch.
- **Auditing & Reporting Models**:
  - `DefectRecord`: Individual defect details (`defect_type`, `origin`, `symbol`, `timestamp`, `details`).
  - `CoverageSummary`: Symbol coverage ratio, total bars, expected bars, gap count, duplicate count.
  - `DataQualityReport`: Overall dataset audit summary with JSON and structured dictionary export.
- **Verification via Seeded Defects**:
  - Tested against synthetic datasets with deliberately injected defects (missing minutes, duplicated rows, 50% price spikes, holiday bars, 0 volume) confirming 100% detection rate.

## Acceptance scenarios

### B1 — Gap detection with calendar awareness
- Detects missing 1m bars during trading session while ignoring lunch breaks, weekends, and holidays.

### B2 — Duplicate timestamp detection
- Identifies duplicate bars and isolates affected timestamps.

### B3 — Price outlier and extreme anomaly detection
- Flags single-bar 25% price spikes while accepting normal market volatility.

### B4 — Zero volume and flat bar detection
- Identifies zero volume candles during regular trading hours.

### B5 — Upstream vs Warehouse error classification
- Correctly tags raw broker omissions as UPSTREAM_SOURCE and duplicate ingestions as WAREHOUSE_INTEGRITY.
