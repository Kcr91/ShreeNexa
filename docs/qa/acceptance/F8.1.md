# F8.1 Acceptance Contract — Black-76 Pricing, Forward Selection, Brent IV Solver, Greeks, and Conventions

## Feature

Black-76 pricing model, forward price selection, Brent IV solver with vega guard, closed-form Greeks, convention parameters (ACT/365, calendar hours to 15:30 IST), reliability flags, and vector/incremental forms.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Black-76 Forward-Based Pricing Model (Spec §11.3)**:
  - Correct model for Indian index options (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY) which settle against futures/forward.
  - Forward $F$, Strike $K$, Time to expiry $T$ (years), Risk-free rate $r$, Volatility $\sigma$:
    $$d_1 = \frac{\ln(F/K) + (\sigma^2 / 2)T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}$$
    $$d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T}$$
    $$\text{Call} = e^{-rT} [F \cdot N(d_1) - K \cdot N(d_2)]$$
    $$\text{Put} = e^{-rT} [K \cdot N(-d_2) - F \cdot N(-d_1)]$$
- **Forward Selection Hierarchy (Spec §11.3)**:
  1. Actual Futures LTP if available and liquid (`ForwardSource.FUTURES_LTP`).
  2. Synthetic forward derived from ATM Put-Call Parity ($F = K + e^{rT}(C_{\text{ATM}} - P_{\text{ATM}})$) (`ForwardSource.SYNTHETIC_PCP`).
  3. Cost-of-carry spot forward ($F = S \cdot e^{(r - q)T}$) (`ForwardSource.SPOT_COC`).
- **Brent IV Inversion Solver & Near-Zero Vega Guard**:
  - Brent root-finding method bracketed on $\sigma \in [0.001, 5.0]$ solving $\text{Black76}(\sigma) - P_{\text{mkt}} = 0$.
  - Vega guard: if $T \le 0$ or vega is near zero ($< 10^{-5}$), marks `is_iv_reliable = False` with explicit `unreliable_reason = "Vega near zero: IV numerically unreliable"` instead of reporting a fabricated number.
  - Intrinsic guard: marks `is_iv_reliable = False` if market price is below intrinsic value.
- **Closed-Form Greeks & Indian Conventions**:
  - Delta: Call $e^{-rT} N(d_1) \in [0, 1]$, Put $-e^{-rT} N(-d_1) \in [-1, 0]$.
  - Gamma: $\frac{e^{-rT} N'(d_1)}{F \sigma \sqrt{T}} \ge 0$.
  - Theta: 1-day calendar decay (scaled by $1/365$ or $1/252$).
  - Vega: 1-vol-point sensitivity (scaled by $0.01$).
  - Rho: 1-rate-point sensitivity (scaled by $0.01$).
- **Vectorized & Incremental Forms**:
  - `price_black76_vector`: fast batch pricing and Greek calculation for numpy arrays of strikes/expiries.
  - `price_black76_scalar`: fast single-contract scalar computation.
- **Proof Requirements**:
  - Parity with hand-calculated and standard `py_vollib` test vectors within $10^{-5}$.
  - Put-call parity exact match: $\text{Call} - \text{Put} = e^{-rT}(F - K)$.
  - Greek bounds: Call Delta $\ge 0$, Put Delta $\le 0$, Gamma $\ge 0$, Vega $\ge 0$.
  - Near-zero vega and below-intrinsic cases reliably flagged.
