# F8.2 Acceptance Contract — Dhan-Chain Calibration, Convention Fitting, Tolerance Policy, Persistence, and Drift Badge

## Feature

Continuous Dhan option chain calibration routine, convention fitting against broker published Greeks (especially theta), tolerance policy, persistent storage, REST API, and frontend drift badge.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Continuous Calibration Engine (Spec §11.2 & §11.4)**:
  - Fits convention parameters (Forward source, Day count 365 vs 252, Time-to-expiry mode, Risk-free rate) to reproduce Dhan's published option chain Greeks.
  - **Theta convention acceptance requirement**: Theta is where conventions bite hardest; calibration explicitly ensures Theta agreement on at least 20 liquid strikes.
- **Unreliable Quote Exclusion Policy (Spec §11.3 & §11.4)**:
  - Excludes unreliable quotes from fitting with explicit reasons:
    - `VEGA_NEAR_ZERO`: Near-zero vega contracts ($< 10^{-5}$) where IV is numerically meaningless.
    - `WIDE_SPREAD`: Bid-ask spread $> 25\%$ or zero bid.
    - `ZERO_LIQUIDITY`: Zero open interest or volume.
    - `BELOW_INTRINSIC`: Market price below discounted intrinsic value.
    - `DEEP_OTM_ITM`: Moneyness $| \ln(F/K) | > 0.15$ with minimal time value.
- **Tolerance Policy & Drift Thresholds**:
  - `CALIBRATED` (Green): Theta RMSE $< 0.50$ INR / $< 8\%$ relative drift.
  - `WARNING` (Amber): Minor drift ($8\% \le \text{drift} < 15\%$).
  - `DRIFT_DETECTED` (Red): Significant divergence ($\ge 15\%$) requiring recalibration.
- **Reconciliation Proof**:
  - Reconciles at least 20 liquid strikes with exact error metrics recorded.
- **Persistence & API**:
  - In-memory / Redis cache storing calibrated conventions by underlying.
  - REST endpoints:
    - `GET /api/v1/options/calibration/{underlying}`
    - `POST /api/v1/options/calibration/calibrate`
- **Frontend Drift Badge**:
  - `DriftBadge.tsx` component in Option Chain toolbar.
  - Displays calibrated status (`🟢 Calibrated`, `🟡 Minor Drift`, `🔴 Drift Detected`), Theta error, and popover breakdown.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/analytics/calibration.py` — Calibration models, tolerance policy, exclusion filters, and grid fitting routine.
