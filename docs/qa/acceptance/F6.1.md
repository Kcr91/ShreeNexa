# F6.1 Acceptance Contract — Multi-Strategy Capital Allocation and Portfolio Run Orchestration

## Feature

Multi-strategy capital allocation engine, isolated strategy books, no double-spend guarantees, deterministic portfolio rebalancing, and synchronized multi-asset backtest run orchestration.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Strict Allocation Invariant**:
  - Strategy allocation weights must be strictly positive ($w_i > 0$) and sum to 1.0 within numerical tolerance ($|1.0 - \sum w_i| \le 10^{-6}$).
  - Initial capital per strategy $C_i = C_{\text{total}} \times w_i$ strictly satisfies $\sum C_i = C_{\text{total}}$.
  - Rejects malformed allocation lists (empty, non-positive weights, or non-unity sums).
- **Isolated Strategy Books**:
  - Each strategy runs inside an independent `StrategyBook` maintaining its own cash ledger, positions, realized PnL, unrealized PnL, and `DailyPnLTracker`.
  - Zero cross-contamination: strategy A can never spend, lock, or draw margin from strategy B's allocated capital.
- **Capital Conservation & No Double-Spend**:
  - During rebalancing, capital transfers across books conserve funds: $\sum \Delta \text{cash}_i = 0$ (no money created or lost).
  - Cash transfers are recorded as external cash flows in each book's `DailyPnLTracker`, preserving accurate Time-Weighted Return (TWR) and Money-Weighted Return (MWR).
- **Deterministic Rebalancing**:
  - Rebalance triggers (calendar-based: Daily/Weekly/Monthly, or drift threshold-based: $|\Delta w_i| \ge \theta$) yield byte-identical results given identical market bars and seeds.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/portfolio/allocation.py` — Allocation validator, capital splitter, and invariant checker.
2. `backend/app/portfolio/book.py` — `StrategyBook` providing strict isolation of cash, positions, and `DailyPnLTracker`.
3. `backend/app/portfolio/orchestrator.py` — Multi-strategy portfolio backtest orchestrator with synchronized timeline replay and deterministic rebalancing.
4. `backend/app/portfolio/models.py` — Portfolio allocation, rebalancing, and execution models.
5. `backend/app/portfolio/__init__.py` — Package exports.
6. `backend/tests/unit/test_portfolio_orchestrator.py` — Comprehensive unit and invariant tests.
7. `docs/qa/acceptance/F6.1.md` — Acceptance contract.

## Acceptance scenarios

### A1 — Allocation invariant and validation
- Validates weights summing to 1.0.
- Rejects non-unity weights, zero or negative weights, and empty allocations with explicit validation errors.
- Proves $\sum C_i = C_{\text{total}}$ to floating point precision.

### A2 — Isolated strategy books and no double-spend
- Verifies orders and fills in book 1 affect only book 1's cash and positions.
- Verifies total portfolio cash = sum of individual book cash amounts at every step.

### A3 — Deterministic rebalancing with zero-sum transfers
- Triggers rebalancing via calendar and drift threshold.
- Asserts $\sum \Delta \text{cash}_i = 0$ across all participating books.
- Verifies sub-strategy `DailyPnLTracker` accounts for cash flows without distorting TWR.

### A4 — Replay determinism
- Rerunning identical portfolio backtest produces bit-exact identical equity curves, trades, and rebalance events.
