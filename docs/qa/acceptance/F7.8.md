# F7.8 Acceptance Contract — Index and Constituent Heatmaps with Breadth

## Feature

Index and constituent heatmaps with market breadth, sentiment measures, transparent weighting source, and deterministic missing-weight handling.

## Scope decisions recorded before implementation

- **Two-Level Heatmap Architecture (Spec §12.6)**:
  - **Index-level View**: Across major NSE/BSE sectoral indices (NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT, NIFTY AUTO, NIFTY PHARMA, NIFTY FMCG, NIFTY METAL, NIFTY ENERGY, NIFTY REALTY, NIFTY 50, NIFTY NEXT 50). Sized by market importance, coloured by percentage change, with futures basis and OI change.
  - **Constituent Drill-In View**: Underlying stocks of any selected index sized proportionally to index weight, coloured by price percentage change.
- **Sentiment and Market Breadth Measures**:
  - `advances`: count of constituents with `changePct > 0`.
  - `declines`: count of constituents with `changePct < 0`.
  - `unchanged`: count of constituents with `changePct == 0`.
  - `advance_decline_ratio`: $\text{advances} / \max(\text{declines}, 1)$.
  - `pct_above_prev_close`: percentage of constituents trading above previous close ($(\text{advances}/\text{total}) \times 100$).
  - `weighted_breadth`: $\sum (\text{weight}_i \times \text{changePct}_i) / 100$.
  - Posture badge: "Strong Bullish", "Moderate Bullish", "Neutral", "Moderate Bearish", "Strong Bearish".
- **Transparent Weighting Source & Deterministic Missing Weight Handling (Core Invariant)**:
  - Every constituent cell displays its weighting source (`OFFICIAL_NSE`, `FREE_FLOAT_MCAP`, or `FALLBACK_EQUAL_WEIGHT`).
  - Missing weights are never silently omitted or zeroed out:
    - If weight is missing or null, remaining percentage $(100 - \sum \text{known\_weights})$ is distributed equally among unweighted constituents.
    - Missing weight cells are visibly labelled: `[Missing Weight - Fallback Assigned]`.
    - Total normalized weights across all constituent cells strictly sum to $100.0\%$.
- **Seamless Interactive Drill-In**:
  - Clicking an index tile in the Index View seamlessly drills down into that index's constituent heatmap.

## Required deliverables

1. `backend/app/api/heatmap.py` — REST endpoints for `/api/v1/heatmap/indices` and `/api/v1/heatmap/{index_name}/constituents`.
2. `backend/app/main.py` — Mount `heatmap_router`.
3. `frontend/src/heatmap/types.ts` — TypeScript models for heatmap items, breadths, weighting sources.
4. `frontend/src/heatmap/engine.ts` — Mathematical breadth calculation, deterministic missing weight assignment, color scaling.
5. `frontend/src/heatmap/engine.test.ts` — Verification of breadth math, cell total reconciliation, and deterministic missing-weight labelling.
6. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/MarketHeatmapWidget.tsx` — Two-level interactive heatmap widget with breadth strip and drill-in.
7. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/MarketHeatmapWidget.test.tsx` — Component tests for level switching, breadth rendering, and cell drill-in.
8. `frontend/src/widgets/builtin/index.ts` — Register `marketHeatmapDefinition`.
9. `docs/qa/acceptance/F7.8.md` — Acceptance contract.
